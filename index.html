About this tracker

A personal learning companion for becoming an SAP Technical Architect / Cloud Engineer — covering ECC → S/4HANA migration, RISE with SAP, SAP BTP, hyperscaler infrastructure, and DevOps practice.

Private by design

Progress and your custom links are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent to a server.

Back it up

Use Export to download your progress and links as JSON, and Import to restore them on another device or browser.

Make it yours

Curated links ship with the roadmap in data.js; your own links live per skill via “+ Add link” on the My Progress tab.
